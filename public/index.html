Biography

Dr. Rana Humayun Babar

Endoscopic Sinus & Cosmetic Nose Surgeon

Dr. Rana Humayun Babar is a highly qualified ENT specialist based in Kabirwala. With a strong foundation in medicine and advanced surgical training, he has dedicated his career to providing exceptional ENT care to patients across the region.

As an Otorhinolaryngologist, Dr. Humayun Babar specializes in endoscopic sinus surgery and cosmetic nose procedures, combining precision with an artistic eye to deliver outstanding results.

Dr. Humayun Babar is deeply committed to continuous medical education and stays current with the latest advances in ENT medicine and surgery. He believes in treating not just the condition but the whole person, taking time to understand each patient's unique concerns and goals.

At Wajid ENT Clinic, he has created a warm, welcoming environment where patients from Kabirwala, Jhang, and surrounding areas receive world-class ENT care close to home.
